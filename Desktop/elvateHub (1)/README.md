ElevateHub — Tech Mentorship & Career Development Platform
🌟 Overview

ElevateHub is a professional mentorship and career development platform designed specifically for the Arab tech community. The platform connects students, fresh graduates, mentors, and employers in one centralized ecosystem to support career growth, knowledge sharing, and professional networking.

Unlike global platforms that lack regional focus, ElevateHub addresses the unique needs of the Middle East tech ecosystem, including cultural considerations, local job opportunities, and a dedicated Women in Tech safe space.

🎯 Project Objectives
Bridge the gap between students and industry professionals
Support career development for fresh graduates
Provide mentorship opportunities within the Arab tech community
Create a safe and inclusive environment for Women in Tech
Centralize learning resources, job opportunities, and networking
